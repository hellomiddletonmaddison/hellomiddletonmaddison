About

A professional, fully documented approach to book marketing

Clear positioning, honest strategy, and consistent execution, communicated entirely in writing.

I'm Maddison Middleton, and I specialize in book marketing for authors and publishers who expect a professional, dependable process rather than vague promises or informal conversations that are difficult to track.

Every project I take on runs entirely through email. That means every brief, every strategy document, every update, and every deliverable exists in writing from the very first message you send. Nothing is left to memory, and nothing depends on a phone call or meeting that only one person can accurately recall later. If you ever need to review what was agreed, what was sent, or what remains outstanding, the complete record is in your inbox.

I have been working in book marketing for several years, helping authors and publishers move a finished manuscript from written to read. My approach is built on three principles: clear positioning, honest strategy, and consistent execution. These are the fundamentals that genuinely move books, rather than shortcuts, gimmicks, or inflated guarantees.

I think about book marketing the same way an editor thinks about a manuscript: nothing gets sent out until it has been reviewed, tightened, and checked against what the reader actually needs. That means a pitch is not sent until it is genre appropriate and specific to the outlet, an ad is not scaled up until its early results justify the spend, and a launch plan is not finalized until the timeline has been checked against the realities of your release date.

Clients who work well with me tend to value the same things I do: clarity over guesswork, a written record over a verbal promise, and a marketing plan that is honest about what a book can realistically achieve in its category.

Professional standards I hold myself to

  • Every scope of work is confirmed in writing before any project begins.
  • Every deliverable is reviewed for quality before it is sent to you.
  • Every result reported to you is verifiable and honestly represented.
  • Every timeline agreed upon is treated as a professional commitment.

Why an email only process

  • A complete written record for every decision, so nothing depends on memory.
  • No scheduling friction. You can send project details whenever it suits you, without coordinating calendars or time zones.
  • Clear accountability. Deliverables, deadlines, and feedback are documented and time stamped.
  • Full traceability. Every past conversation remains searchable in your inbox, so nothing needs to be repeated or reconstructed.

Genres and project focus

I take on projects across [genre or category focus], and I am selective about the number of projects I run at once, so that each one receives a properly written, considered plan rather than a rushed template.
