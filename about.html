About Maddison Middleton

A professional, fully documented approach to book marketing

Clear positioning, honest strategy, and consistent execution, communicated entirely in writing. Based in New York, USA.

I help move finished manuscripts from written to read.

I'm Maddison Middleton, a book marketing specialist based in New York, USA. I specialize in book marketing for authors and publishers who expect a professional, dependable process rather than vague promises or informal conversations that are difficult to track.

Every project I take on runs entirely through email. That means every brief, every strategy document, every update, and every deliverable exists in writing from the very first message you send. Nothing is left to memory, and nothing depends on a phone call or meeting that only one person can accurately recall later. If you ever need to review what was agreed, what was sent, or what remains outstanding, the complete record is in your inbox.

My approach is built on three principles: clear positioning, honest strategy, and consistent execution. These are the fundamentals that genuinely move books, rather than shortcuts, gimmicks, or inflated guarantees.

I think about book marketing the same way an editor thinks about a manuscript: nothing gets sent out until it has been reviewed, tightened, and checked against what the reader actually needs. That means a pitch is not sent until it is genre-appropriate and specific to the outlet, an ad is not scaled up until its early results justify the spend, and a launch plan is not finalized until the timeline has been checked against the realities of your release date.

Why an Email-Only Process Serves You Better

  • A complete written record: Every decision and deliverable is documented for easy reference.
  • No scheduling friction: Send project details whenever suits you without juggling calendar slots or time zones.
  • Clear accountability: Deliverables, deadlines, and feedback are time-stamped.
  • Full traceability: Search past conversations easily in your inbox.
