Guiding Principles

My Foundational Marketing Philosophies

Effective book marketing is built on practical fundamentals, not fleeting internet tricks.

1. Algorithms Respond to Clear Signals

Many authors view recommendation systems on Amazon, BookBub, and Goodreads as unpredictable obstacles. In reality, retail algorithms are simply automated systems designed to connect the right product with the right buyer. When we structure your backend keywords, subcategories, and blurbs with clear, unambiguous genre signals, the algorithm becomes an effective tool for growing your audience.

2. Strong Positioning Precedes Paid Traffic

Spending advertising dollars on an unoptimized book page with a weak blurb and generic packaging leads to expensive, wasted clicks. If your cover, subtitle, and description fail to immediately communicate your book's core appeal, visitors will bounce. I focus on building a strong conversion foundation before recommending any paid marketing spend.

3. Respect Reader Search Intent

When readers are ready to buy, they look for specific reading experiences. A reader rarely searches for "a wonderful emotional journey"; they search for "small-town holiday romance" or "space opera with military politics." Matching your metadata to real search intent makes finding and buying your book simple for readers.

4. Catalog-Level Strategy Over Single-Book Thinking

A book is not an isolated product—it is part of an author's broader catalog. We build marketing systems that promote your backlist, increase series read-through rates, and guide readers from your first release toward your entire body of work.

Category Knowledge

Submarket Knowledge Across 15+ Genres

Different genres have different reader habits. Here is how our approach adapts across major categories.

Romance & Subgenres

Contemporary, Regency, Dark Romance, Fantasy Romance, Paranormal, Romantic Suspense.

Romance readers look for specific tropes, pairing dynamics, and heat levels. We optimize metadata around core reader expectations (e.g., enemies-to-lovers, forced proximity, second-chance romance) to attract dedicated genre fans.

Science Fiction & Fantasy

Epic Fantasy, Space Opera, Cozy Fantasy, LitRPG, Cyberpunk, Urban Fantasy, Hard Sci-Fi.

Speculative fiction requires distinct subgenre signaling. We ensure packaging clearly differentiates cozy, character-driven stories from expansive, fast-paced adventures or hard sci-fi.

Mystery, Thriller & Suspense

Psychological Thrillers, Cozy Mysteries, Legal Suspense, Police Procedurals, Noir.

Suspense readers respond to strong tension and high stakes. We craft marketing copy around central dilemmas that encourage readers to sample the opening chapters immediately.

Nonfiction, Memoir & Business

Personal Development, Business Strategy, Professional Memoirs, Health & Wellness, Leadership.

Nonfiction requires clear authority and immediate utility. We structure subtitles, blurbs, and A+ Content around specific solutions to build trust with professional readers.
