Author M. • Fantasy Romance

Launch Campaign & Category Positioning

Scope of Work: 60-day launch campaign, metadata restructuring, and blurb copywriting.

Approach: Identified high-intent search strings used by fantasy romance readers. Repositioned the title out of broad fantasy into specific fairy tale retelling subcategories.

We combined reviewer outreach with targeted genre promotions. By positioning the book within precise subcategories, we helped it gain organic visibility among readers seeking those exact tropes.

Verifiable Outcomes:

  • Selected by 3 active indie book clubs
  • Review count doubled within 60 days of launch
  • BSR improved by over 400,000 places
Author D. • Thriller Series

Series Read-Through Funnel Overhaul

Scope of Work: Read-through audit across a 3-book thriller series, back-matter CTA updates, and Book 2 blurb rewrite.

Approach: Placed direct 1-click buy links at the end of Book 1 and revised the Book 2 description to focus on the continuing plot tension.

We audited the transition between Book 1 and Book 2. Clean purchase links and a clear series connection made continuing the series an easy choice for readers.

Verifiable Outcomes:

  • Series read-through rate rose from 20% to 52%
  • Total monthly series royalties nearly tripled
  • Cost per acquisition decreased significantly
