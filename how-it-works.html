100% Written Process

Five Steps, Every One Confirmed In Writing

A documented workflow built around clarity, time-stamped decisions, and zero calendar friction.

01

Send an Email

Reach out with a description of your book, genre, release timeline, and the support you need. There are no consultation booking widgets or scheduling required—simply send a written message describing your goals.

By focusing entirely on written communication, you can describe your book's themes, pen names, and marketing hopes without the pressure of a real-time call.

02

Written Proposal & Scope

You will receive a detailed written proposal outlining the recommended approach, full scope of work, timeline, and itemized cost. You can ask questions and request adjustments over email until the proposal reflects exactly what you need.

This step ensures both parties are completely aligned on all goals, pricing details, and timeline milestones before any agreement is made.

03

Project Kickoff Brief

Once the proposal is approved, work begins. You receive an initial project kickoff brief by email confirming all deliverables and deadlines, establishing a clear reference point from day one.

This brief acts as our project blueprint, letting you know exactly when to expect draft reviews and final campaigns.

04

Regular Written Updates

Throughout the project, you receive structured progress updates, draft reviews, and performance summaries by email on an agreed schedule. Every important detail is recorded in writing.

You remain fully informed at every stage of the project, with clear data updates sent straight to your inbox.

05

Delivery & Wrap-Up Summary

At the conclusion of the project, you receive a final summary email covering everything delivered, along with professional recommendations for your book's next steps.

This file serves as your permanent marketing reference, providing a complete record of what was delivered and how to maintain your sales momentum.
