How It Works

A clear, fully documented process

Five steps, every one of them confirmed in writing.

01

Send an email

Reach out with a description of your book and the kind of support you are looking for. There are no forms to book a call and no scheduling required, simply a written message.

02

Written proposal

You will receive a detailed, written proposal by email outlining the recommended approach, full scope of work, timeline, and cost. You can ask questions and request adjustments over email until the proposal reflects exactly what you need.

03

Project kickoff

Once the proposal is approved, work begins. You will receive an initial project brief by email confirming everything that has been agreed, establishing a clear reference point from day one.

04

Regular written updates

Throughout the project, you will receive progress updates, drafts, and performance summaries by email, on a schedule agreed upfront. Every important detail is communicated in writing, never verbally only.

05

Delivery and wrap up

At the conclusion of the project, you will receive a final summary email covering everything delivered, along with professional recommendations for next steps.

Start with a single email

Tell me about your book and your goals. You will hear back with a clear, written next step.
