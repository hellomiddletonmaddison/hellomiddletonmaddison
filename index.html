Who this is for

Book marketing works differently depending on where you are in the process. Here is how support typically breaks down.

  • Authors preparing for launch

    You have a release date and need a coordinated plan across advertising, media, and reader outreach, documented clearly enough that nothing falls through the cracks in the run up to release week.

  • Authors with a published backlist

    Your book is already out, but its marketing has gone quiet. Renewed advertising, Listopia placement, and book club outreach can bring a title back into active discovery.

  • Small presses and independent publishers

    You are managing marketing across multiple titles and need a specialist who can take ownership of specific campaigns and report back in writing, without adding meetings to your schedule.

  • Authors who want a written paper trail

    You want to know exactly what was proposed, what was delivered, and what it achieved, without relying on a call you cannot easily revisit later.

A book does not sell itself the moment it is published. It sells when the right readers are shown it, at the right time, in the right place, again and again.
