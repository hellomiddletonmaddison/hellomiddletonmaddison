Comprehensive Support

Marketing Services for Every Stage of Your Book's Lifecycle

Every project begins with a clear, written scope of work and fixed pricing delivered straight to your inbox. No calls required.

🚀

01. Launch Campaigns

A structured, written marketing roadmap covering the entire launch arc, from pre-order setup through release week promotion to post-launch momentum.

I design a timeline tailored to your target audience, coordinating promotions, review outreach, and advertising to build launch momentum.

  • Custom launch timeline mapped to release date
  • Target reader profile identification
  • Promotional stacking schedule
  • Weekly written progress updates
⚡

02. Metadata Optimization

Deep restructuring of your book's backend KDP search strings, subcategory node paths, and retail indexing to improve search discovery.

We align your retail data with high-intent search queries, helping your title appear in front of readers who are actively looking for your genre.

  • High-intent search query research
  • 7 targeted 50-character search strings
  • Subcategory node mapping & reassignment
  • Step-by-step implementation guide
📊

03. Advertising Management

Setup, management, and optimization of advertising campaigns across Amazon Ads, Meta Ads, and BookBub.

I build targeted ad campaigns focused on relevant comp titles and keywords, managing spend to improve sales conversion.

  • Comp author & keyword targeting
  • Custom ad copy & image testing
  • Budget & bid optimization
  • Bi-weekly written performance summaries
📰

04. Media & Reviewer Outreach

Personalized, written pitch letters sent to book bloggers, genre reviewers, podcasts, and niche media outlets on your behalf.

We identify and contact curated media targets, presenting your book to reviewers who actively cover your genre.

  • Curated media & reviewer contact list
  • Tailored pitch copy per recipient
  • Shared outreach tracking log
  • Structured follow-up schedule
✍️

05. Marketing Copywriting

Professional blurb copywriting, back-cover text, series summaries, and email sequences designed to engage readers.

I write compelling marketing copy that highlights your book's emotional hooks and core themes to encourage purchases.

  • High-conversion retail blurb drafts
  • Back-matter call-to-action copy
  • Formatted HTML for Amazon product pages
  • Included revision rounds
🎨

06. Book Mockups & Visuals

High-resolution 3D book mockups, flat-lay graphics, and promotional banners designed for your website and social media.

We produce clean visual assets that give your book a professional look across digital and print channels.

  • 3D paperback, hardcover & digital renders
  • Lifestyle and flat-lay scene styling
  • Formatted for web, newsletter, and ads
  • Delivered in high-resolution PNG/JPG
🎬

07. Book Trailers

Short, engaging video trailers built from an approved script to give potential readers a feel for your book's premise.

We create atmospheric trailers formatted for social media feeds, vertical reels, and Amazon Author Central pages.

  • Written concept script & storyboard
  • Licensed cinematic visuals & soundtrack
  • Horizontal (16:9) & vertical (9:16) versions
  • Revisions based on your feedback
⭐

08. Goodreads Listopia

Strategic research and placement of your book across active Goodreads Listopia lists relevant to your genre.

We locate and highlight your title on popular lists, building organic discoverability among dedicated reader communities.

  • Genre-specific list research
  • List placement recommendations
  • Performance tracking summary
  • Organic discovery guidance
📚

09. Book Club Outreach

Direct, written outreach to independent book clubs and reading groups looking for their next selection.

We pitch your book to active reading groups, pairing your story with helpful discussion questions for meetings.

  • Curated book club directory outreach
  • Custom reading group discussion guide
  • Written outreach progress report
  • Reader feedback summaries
