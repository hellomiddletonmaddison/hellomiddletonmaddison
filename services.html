Marketing Support For Every Stage Of Your Book's Life

01. Launch Campaigns

A complete, written marketing plan covering the full arc of your launch, from pre-order buildup through release week promotion to post-launch momentum.

  • Written launch timeline mapped to release date
  • Target reader audience identification
  • Promotional asset coordination
  • Weekly written progress updates

02. Platform & Positioning

Shaping how you present yourself across social media, your newsletter, and website so your brand communicates clearly.

  • Written brand positioning summary
  • Messaging guidelines for consistent tone

  • Ongoing written feedback

Setup, launch, and ongoing management of advertising campaigns across Amazon Ads, Meta Ads, and BookBub.

  • Written performance reports
  • Budget recommendations

Carefully written pitches crafted and sent on your behalf to book bloggers, reviewers, podcasts, and genre outlets.

  • Individually written pitches
  • Written outreach tracking log
  • Systematic follow-ups

05. Marketing Copywriting

Professional copywriting for blurbs, back cover copy, email sequences, and promotional content built to convert.

  • Draft based on creative brief
  • Structured written revision rounds
  • Final polished copy delivery

06. Book Mockups & Visuals

Professionally designed mockups of your cover, including 3D renders, flat-lay styling, and lifestyle staged images.

  • Revisions based on written input

  • Social, web & ad formats
