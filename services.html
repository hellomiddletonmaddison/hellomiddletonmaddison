Services

Comprehensive marketing support for every stage of your book's life

Every service begins with a written scope of work, so you know exactly what is included before anything starts.

Services can be booked individually or combined into a single campaign, depending on where your book is in its life cycle.

01

Launch campaigns

A complete, written marketing plan covering the full arc of your launch, from pre order buildup through release week promotion to post launch momentum.

  • Written launch timeline mapped to your release date
  • Identification of target reader audiences and platforms
  • Coordination of promotional assets across the campaign
  • Weekly written progress updates during the active launch window

Ideal for: Authors and publishers with a confirmed release date who need a coordinated, full scope campaign.

02

Author platform and brand positioning

Support in shaping how you present yourself across social media, your newsletter, and your website, so your presence clearly and consistently communicates who you are.

  • A written brand positioning summary
  • Messaging guidelines for consistent tone across platforms
  • Content direction for social media and newsletters
  • Ongoing written feedback as your platform develops

Ideal for: Authors whose online presence feels scattered or inconsistent across platforms.

03

Advertising campaign management

Setup, launch, and ongoing management of advertising campaigns across Amazon Ads, Meta Ads, and BookBub.

  • Full campaign setup and audience targeting
  • Ongoing optimization based on performance data
  • Written performance reports on a set schedule
  • Budget recommendations based on measurable results

Ideal for: Authors who want their ad spend actively managed and reported on, not left to run unattended.

04

Media and publicity outreach

Carefully written pitches, crafted and sent on your behalf to book bloggers, reviewers, podcasts, and media contacts relevant to your genre.

  • A curated, genre relevant media contact list
  • Individually written pitches for each outlet
  • A written log of outreach sent and responses received
  • Follow up outreach where appropriate

Ideal for: Authors seeking reviews, features, or press coverage ahead of or following a release.

05

Marketing copywriting

Professional copywriting for blurbs, back cover copy, email sequences, and promotional content, written to turn a browsing reader into a buyer.

  • Initial draft based on a written creative brief
  • Structured rounds of written revisions
  • Final polished copy delivered in your preferred format

Ideal for: Authors who need blurbs, back cover copy, or promotional writing that reads as professionally as the book itself.

06

Book mockups

Professionally designed mockups of your book cover, including 3D renders, flat lay styling, and lifestyle staged images.

  • Multiple mockup styles, including 3D render, flat lay, and lifestyle staging
  • Files formatted for web, social media, and print use
  • Revisions based on your written feedback

Ideal for: Authors who need polished visual assets for advertising, social media, or a press kit.

07

Book trailers

Short, professionally planned promotional videos built to give potential readers a genuine feel for your book's tone, world, and central hook.

  • Written script and concept outline for approval
  • Professionally produced trailer video
  • Delivery in formats suited to social media, your website, and paid advertising

Ideal for: Authors who want a short, shareable video asset to support a launch or ongoing promotion.

08

Goodreads Listopia campaigns

Strategic identification and promotion of your book across Goodreads Listopia lists relevant to your genre and themes.

  • Research into the most relevant Listopia lists for your genre
  • A written outreach and placement strategy
  • Ongoing monitoring and reporting on list performance

Ideal for: Authors looking to build organic, reader driven visibility on Goodreads.

09

Book club outreach

Direct, professional, written outreach to book clubs, reading groups, and community organizers to get your book considered for their reading lists.

  • A tailored book club pitch document
  • A professionally prepared discussion guide
  • Outreach to relevant book club communities
  • A written summary of responses and outcomes

Ideal for: Authors hoping to build word of mouth through reading groups and community discussion.

Pricing

Packages start at a rate confirmed once your scope is defined. Every project begins with a written quote and a clearly defined scope of work sent by email, so you know the full cost and deliverables before committing. No consultation call is required to receive a quote.
